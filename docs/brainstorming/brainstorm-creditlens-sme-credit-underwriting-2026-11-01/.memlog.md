[decision] mode set to facilitator
[technique] started Job to Be Done
[idea] Loan officer's JTBD: transfer the burden of proof from the person to the system — defensible decision with audit trail (model score, SHAP, circular citation, threshold)
[idea] SME owner's JTBD: translate real business activity into a language the bank finally understands — feel heard, assessed fairly, given dignity and a reason to come back
[idea] SME portal can't feel like another form — must show the business back to the owner, explain what's hurting the score, what to fix
[idea] Risk manager's JTBD: compress the feedback loop between market reality and lending policy — know before defaults happen, not after
[idea] PSI drift on cash flow feature = economic shift signal, not just MLOps alert — risk manager needs this as a business tool, not DevOps
[idea] Risk manager nightmare: cluster of loans approved 8 months ago, same sector, missing payments — signal was there all along, just not watched
[technique] started Starbursting
[idea] WHO: model is always advisory, never authoritative — regulatory requirement not product choice
[idea] WHO: loan officer follows score on small loans; risk manager gets involved on large ones
[idea] WHO: threshold is owned by risk manager (4-eyes control); ML engineer proposes, risk manager approves; no one else touches it
[idea] WHO: wrong access destroys the integrity of the defensible decision — executive overrides corrupt the audit trail; self-serve scoring enables gaming; external regulator needs read-only exports not live access
[idea] WHAT: CreditLens stores a behavioral financial fingerprint the SME has never seen summarized anywhere — data rights obligation under GDPR/BCT rules
[idea] WHAT: SHAP explanation is legally required disclosure, not just a nice ML feature
[idea] WHAT: cold-start = "unscoreable" (not a low score, a no-score) — graceful degradation to manual underwriting checklist; never pretend to score on thin data
[idea] WHAT: overrides logged with mandatory reason code + free text, flagged to risk manager, fed into override analytics pipeline — never ingested as model training ground truth
[idea] WHAT: override rate per officer is a bias detection metric — 40% override rate in one direction = model wrong for segment OR officer has a bias problem, both need investigation
[idea] WHY: shadow mode as trust-building protocol — show officer "47 cases you said yes, model said no, 12 showing repayment stress" — model earns seat at table by being right on blind spots
[idea] WHY: self-hosted is the only architecture that keeps data governance promises — SaaS = bank's legal team kills procurement before launch; SME data must not leave bank perimeter
[idea] WHY: uncited RAG answer creates liability — "AI told me it was compliant" is not a legal defense; citation is chain of custody for legal reasoning; uncited answer more dangerous than no answer
[idea] HOW: role-keyed SHAP rendering — loan officer gets plain language summary, analyst gets full waterfall, risk manager gets population aggregates; Keycloak RBAC controls complexity level not just access
[idea] HOW: conflict detection step in reindexing DAG — semantic similarity check flags new chunks that contradict existing guidance; new circular sits in "pending/under review" state until compliance officer approves; never silently overwrites legal guidance
[idea] HOW: immutable decision snapshot frozen at decision time — model version hash, feature values, SHAP output, threshold, RAG citations, officer override reason, all timestamped; append-only audit log; two years later auditor gets complete reconstruction of that exact moment
[idea] WHEN: post-disbursement monitoring is ignored because officer incentive ends at approval not repayment — compensation structure problem not UX problem; route drift alerts to risk manager directly, bypass officer
[idea] WHERE: CreditLens must never be positioned as a replacement — "decision support layer alongside existing process"; what it actually replaces is the Excel scorecard and PDF search, but you never say that during procurement; incumbent vendor protection reflex stalls the project
[technique] started Disney Method
[idea] DREAMER: SME has a living credit profile (financial vital sign) visible on their phone in real time — bank comes to them, not the other way around
[idea] DREAMER: compliance layer reads regulatory updates and rewrites its own guidance without human intervention
[idea] DREAMER: risk manager dashboard predicts portfolio stress from macro signals before it appears in repayment data — adjusts thresholds automatically
[idea] DREAMER: CreditLens becomes the de facto financial identity infrastructure for SMEs — portable identity travels to any bank, any country, any lender
[idea] DREAMER: SME financial passport — SME owns their score and behavioral fingerprint; banks compete for the relationship; two-sided market for SME credit; financial infrastructure at the level of SWIFT but for the 60% SWIFT never reached
[idea] REALIST: v1 SME portal = read-only view of features extracted, score received, SHAP in plain language, BCT articles checked — downloadable PDF financial profile; plants the flag that SME has a right to see what system knows about them
[idea] REALIST: proactive outreach when declined SME profile improves — reuse drift monitoring DAG to watch individual feature vectors cross threshold; fire RabbitMQ event to notify assigned loan officer; no new infrastructure, just a new DAG and notification template
[idea] CRITIC: minimum viable override failure — officers find the fastest reason code and use it every time; audit trail looks complete but is compliance theater; model gets no meaningful signal from override patterns; system designed to detect real reasoning but detects performance of reasoning instead
[idea] CRITIC: semantic promiscuity in RAG — one high-similarity chunk cited in 40% of queries because it uses broad regulatory language; similarity ≠ relevance; RAGAS test set too small and clean to catch frequency bias in production; fix = citation frequency dashboard: any chunk cited above statistical threshold relative to peers triggers human review
[technique] started Backcasting
[idea] BACKCASTING vision: "A Tuesday where the system was invisible because everything it was supposed to prevent simply didn't happen" — 23-minute approval, no spreadsheet opened, no PDF searched, SME gets plain-language explanation of what improved since their last declined application
[idea] BACKCASTING step 1: final dependency before that Tuesday was one officer's private trust — formed through shadow mode showing honest results over time; "you can't deploy trust, you can only create the conditions for it to form"
[idea] BACKCASTING step 2: before trust could form, shadow mode results had to be worth showing — required clean, stable features from real transaction data; model wrong in random ways (bad features) is worse than consistently wrong; features must match the reality the officer sees manually
[idea] BACKCASTING step 3: before clean features, one political decision had to happen — senior bank sign-off on data access agreement defining which transaction fields CreditLens can read, in what format, under whose authorization; first move is not technical, it's getting one signature; everything traces back to that data handshake
[decision] convergence: affinity clustering produced 8 clusters (A Trust Architecture, B Accountability & Auditability, C Graceful Degradation, D Role-keyed Experience, E Incentive Misalignment, F SME Dignity & Rights, G Platform as Infrastructure, H Build Sequence)
[decision] MoSCoW: Must = B + D (skeleton — without these value proposition doesn't exist); Should = A + H (adoption that sticks vs collapses); Could = F + G (real value, require stable core first, v2); Won't = C + E (require production data to tune; org problems outside software scope)
[insight] core through-line: the whole project is about closing the gap between a decision being made and that decision being explainable — SHAP, citation chain, immutable snapshot, role-keyed rendering are all faces of the same problem
[insight] shadow mode is not a testing phase, it's the trust-building protocol — if cut under time pressure the entire adoption strategy collapses; must be designed intentionally from day one
[insight] financial passport is the answer to "why does this project exist beyond a portfolio piece" — started as Dreamer room idea, points toward something that matters at scale for Tunisia and the region
[insight] compliance theater + shadow mode failure are the same problem from opposite ends — both solved only if the system shows people things they couldn't have seen without it; the technical feature is identical, human follow-through is what makes it real
[insight] "unscoreable" state is not an edge case — it's the system's epistemology; willingness to say "insufficient data" earns the authority of scores it does return; intellectual honesty is a design pattern not just an ethical stance; applies equally to RAG low-confidence retrievals
[insight] financial passport (Dreamer) and data access agreement (Backcasting) are the same legal question from opposite sides — bank says "we can use this SME's data"; passport is the SME saying "this data is mine"; v1 and v3 are not different products, they're the same principle at different stages of power redistribution
