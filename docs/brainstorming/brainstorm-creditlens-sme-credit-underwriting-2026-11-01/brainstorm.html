Technique 01
Job to Be Done
Loan Officer
Transfer the burden of proof from the person to the system
  • Defensible decision backed by full audit trail
  • Model score + SHAP + circular citation + threshold
  • Override logged with mandatory reason code — never ignored, never silently discarded
  • Override rate per officer = bias detection metric
SME Owner
Translate real business activity into a language the bank finally understands
  • Feel heard, assessed fairly, given dignity
  • Portal shows the business back to the owner
  • Explains what's hurting the score and what to fix
  • Not another form — a mirror of the business
Risk Manager
Compress the feedback loop between market reality and lending policy
  • Know before defaults happen, not after
  • PSI drift on cash-flow feature = economic shift signal, not just DevOps alert
  • Nightmare: cluster of loans approved 8 months ago, same sector, missing payments — signal was there all along, just not watched
  • Threshold owned 4-eyes: ML engineer proposes, risk manager approves
Technique 02
Starbursting
WHO
  • Model = advisory, never authoritative (regulatory requirement)
  • Loan officer on small loans; risk manager on large
  • Threshold owned 4-eyes: ML proposes, RM approves
  • Wrong access corrupts the audit trail — no executive overrides
WHAT
  • Behavioral financial fingerprint — GDPR/BCT data rights obligation
  • SHAP = legally required disclosure, not a UX feature
  • Cold start = "unscoreable" (no-score, not low-score)
  • Overrides logged, never ingested as model ground truth
WHY
  • Shadow mode = trust-building protocol, not a testing phase
  • Self-hosted only: SaaS kills procurement at legal review
  • Uncited RAG answer = liability; citation = chain of custody
HOW
  • Role-keyed SHAP: plain text / full waterfall / population aggregates
  • Conflict detection in reindexing DAG — pending review before overwrite
  • Immutable decision snapshot: model hash + feature values + threshold + RAG citations
WHEN
  • Post-disbursement monitoring ignored: officer incentive ends at approval
  • Drift alerts routed to risk manager, not officer
  • Compensation structure problem, not UX problem
WHERE
  • Never positioned as replacement — "decision support layer"
  • Actually replaces Excel scorecard and PDF search
  • Don't say that during procurement — incumbent protection stalls projects
Technique 03
Disney Method
✦
The Dreamer
Living credit profile — visible on the SME's phone in real time; bank comes to them, not the other way around
Compliance layer rewrites its own guidance when regulatory updates arrive — without human intervention
Risk manager dashboard predicts portfolio stress from macro signals before repayment data reflects it
Financial passport — SME owns their score; banks compete for the relationship; two-sided credit market; the infrastructure SWIFT never reached
⊕
The Realist
v1 SME portal = read-only view: extracted features, score, plain-language SHAP, BCT articles checked — downloadable PDF financial profile
Plants the flag that the SME has a right to see what the system knows about them
Proactive outreach when declined SME's profile improves — reuse drift monitoring DAG, fire RabbitMQ event to notify loan officer; no new infrastructure
⚠
The Critic
Minimum viable override failure — officers find fastest reason code, use every time; audit trail looks complete but is compliance theater; system detects performance of reasoning, not actual reasoning
Semantic promiscuity in RAG — one high-similarity chunk cited in 40% of queries; similarity ≠ relevance; fix = citation frequency dashboard; chunks above statistical threshold trigger human review
Technique 04
Backcasting
A Tuesday where the system was invisible because everything it was supposed to prevent simply didn't happen — 23-minute approval, no spreadsheet opened, no PDF searched, SME gets plain-language explanation of what improved since their last declined application.
The Vision · The Tuesday Test
Step 3 · First Move
One Signature on a Data Access Agreement
Senior bank sign-off defining which transaction fields CreditLens can read, in what format, under whose authorization. The first move is not technical — it is getting this one signature. Every subsequent step traces back to this data handshake.
Step 2 · Foundation
Clean, Stable Features from Real Transaction Data
Shadow mode results must be worth showing before trust can form. A model wrong in random ways (bad features) is worse than consistently wrong. Features must match the reality the officer sees when working manually.
Step 1 · Prerequisite
One Officer's Private Trust
Shadow mode shows honest results over time. Not deployed by announcement — formed through demonstrated accuracy on blind spots the officer already suspected. Trust is earned on specific cases, not on aggregate statistics.
Vision · The Tuesday
The System is Invisible
23-minute approval. No spreadsheet opened. No PDF searched. SME receives a plain-language explanation of what improved since their last declined application. Every prevention is invisible because every failure was prevented.

Climax · Session Synthesis
What This Session Found
Five insights that reframe the project
  • 01
    The whole project is about one gap. The distance between a decision being made and that decision being explainable. SHAP, citation chain, immutable snapshot, and role-keyed rendering are not four features — they are four faces of the same problem. Build one thing that closes that gap, and everything else follows.
  • 02
    Shadow mode is not a testing phase. It is the trust-building protocol. If cut under time pressure, the entire adoption strategy collapses — not just the rollout schedule. It must be designed as a first-class feature from day one, not retrofitted later. The system earns its seat at the table by being right on the loan officer's blind spots.
  • 03
    The financial passport is the answer to "why does this project exist beyond a portfolio piece." It started as a Dreamer room idea. It points toward something that matters at scale for Tunisia and the entire region — portable SME financial identity for the 60% of businesses SWIFT never reached.
  • 04
    Compliance theater and shadow mode failure are the same problem from opposite ends. Both are solved only if the system shows people things they couldn't have seen without it. The technical feature is identical. Human follow-through is what makes it real or empty. A system that detects the performance of reasoning rather than the reasoning itself has failed its purpose.
  • 05
    "Unscoreable" is not an edge case — it is the system's epistemology. The willingness to say "insufficient data" is what earns the authority of every score the system does return. Intellectual honesty is a design pattern, not an ethical stance. It applies equally to the RAG pipeline's low-confidence retrievals. A system that always answers is less trustworthy than one that sometimes declines to.
Core Through-Line

The data access agreement (Backcasting) and the financial passport (Dreamer) are the same legal question from opposite sides. The bank says "we can use this SME's data." The passport is the SME saying "this data is mine." v1 and v3 are not different products — they are the same principle at different stages of power redistribution. The project's long arc bends from bank infrastructure toward SME sovereignty.
